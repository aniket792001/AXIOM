MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (2022)
PARTIES: Apex Cloud Systems, Inc. ("Vendor") and Global Retail Corp. ("Customer")
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 15, 2022

SECTION 1: SERVICES & PLATFORM ACCESS
Vendor agrees to provide Customer with multi-region cloud infrastructure and database synchronization services.

SECTION 2: PAYMENT & BILLING
All undisputed invoices shall be payable Net 30 days from date of receipt. Any late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

SECTION 3: GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware. All disputes shall be settled via binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.

SECTION 4: LIABILITY & INDEMNIFICATION
Section 4.1 Liability Cap:
Except for breaches of confidentiality under Section 7, in no event shall Vendor's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement exceed $1,000,000 (One Million USD).

Section 4.2 Termination for Convenience:
Either party may terminate this Agreement or any applicable Order Form without cause upon delivering thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party.

SECTION 5: DATA PROTECTION & AUDIT
Vendor shall maintain SOC 2 Type II compliance and ISO 27001 certifications. Customer has the right to inspect audit summaries once per calendar year upon twenty (20) business days notice.
